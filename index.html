Made one.

Sarthuk Bloodbane, of the Warsong Clan, carries the memory of an older Horde — an age of blood, steel, broken oaths, and hard-won honor.

His story reaches back to the wars that shaped the orcs. He lived through the shadow of demon blood, fought across the Second and Third Wars, and witnessed the turning point that came with Mannoroth's fall.

He walked his own path before finding his place again within Thrall's Horde, eventually standing among the defenders of Orgrimmar and Azeroth.

“For the Horde. Not for glory. But glory has excellent taste.”
