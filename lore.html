THE LORE

Sarthuk Bloodbane, of the Warsong Clan

BLOOD. HONOR. CHOICE.

Long before the calendar shoots, the dramatic poses and the frankly unreasonable biceps, there was a warrior shaped by the old Horde—and haunted by what it had done.

This is the story of the blood he drank, the blood he refused, and the Horde he chose to defend.

Read the full chronicle ↓

I. Son of the Warsong

Born into the Bloodbane family, Sarthuk was raised by a lieutenant of Grom Hellscream to prize strength, loyalty and glory. When the Warsong followed Grom, he followed too.

II. The Blood Curse

He drank Mannoroth's blood with the other Orcs. On Draenor and during the First and Second Wars, he fought for the old Horde—and began to fear what its fury had made of him.

III. The Choice in Ashenvale

When Grom led his warriors to drink the Pit Lord's blood again, Sarthuk feigned drinking. For the first time, he chose conscience over the path set by Grom Hellscream.

IV. After Mannoroth

Grom's sacrifice broke the curse, but it did not erase memory. After Mount Hyjal, Sarthuk withdrew into Ashenvale to face his past—and decide his future.

V. The Meaning of Bloodbane

In Orgrimmar, Sarthuk pledged himself to Thrall's vision of a different Horde. He kept his family name, but gave it a new meaning: never again would blood rule him.

Sarthuk Bloodbane

Of the Warsong Clan

CHAPTER 01 ✦ Family, loyalty and the weight of a name.

Blood of the Warsong

Sarthuk Bloodbane was born into the Bloodbane family of the Warsong Clan, a fierce and renowned line of warriors whose name carried weight among their people. His father, a respected lieutenant under Grom Hellscream, raised him to believe that strength, loyalty, and glory in battle were the highest virtues an Orc could possess.

From an early age, Sarthuk learned what it meant to be Warsong.

He was taught to fight without hesitation, to endure hardship without complaint, and to follow strength where it led. So when Grom Hellscream's promises of glory, conquest, and honor swept through the Warsong, Sarthuk followed without question, as any son of his bloodline would have.

CHAPTER 02 ✦ When strength became bloodlust.

The First Curse

When the Orc clans drank the Blood of Mannoroth, he drank with them.

The change was immediate. Strength became fury, courage became bloodlust, and battle ceased to be a means to victory. It became an end in itself.

On Draenor, Sarthuk took part in atrocities he would spend the rest of his life remembering.

At the time, he told himself it was war.

Later, he would know better.

He followed the Horde through the Dark Portal and fought during the First and Second Wars, still carrying the Warsong banner. But as the years passed, the rage that had once felt like strength increasingly became something he feared.

Nightmares followed him.

Burning villages. Defeated enemies cut down after the battle was already won. Faces he could no longer remember belonging to people he could never forget.

He could not free himself from Mannoroth's curse, but he slowly learned to resist the urges it planted within him.

After the Horde's defeat in the Second War, many Orcs fell into the strange lethargy that eventually consumed those imprisoned in the human internment camps. The Warsong escaped captivity and continued their war under Grom Hellscream.

For Sarthuk, constant battle was both salvation and punishment.

He never truly stopped fighting long enough to succumb to lethargy, but neither could he escape the memories of what the Horde had become.

CHAPTER 03 ✦ A Warsong warrior learns to say no.

The Choice in Ashenvale

Years later, the Warsong followed Grom west across the Great Sea and into Kalimdor.

In Ashenvale, Sarthuk once again fought at Hellscream's side.

Then came Mannoroth.

Faced with the power of Cenarius, Grom accepted the Pit Lord's blood for a second time and led his warriors to do the same.

Sarthuk could not.

He remembered Draenor.

He remembered what the blood had made of him.

When his brothers drank, Sarthuk only feigned doing the same.

For the first time in his life, he defied the path chosen by Grom Hellscream.

What followed confirmed everything he had feared.

He watched warriors he had fought beside for years surrender themselves completely to the curse. Honor disappeared. Restraint disappeared. There was only rage and the desire to kill.

Sarthuk remained a Warsong warrior, but something fundamental changed within him.

Enemies who surrendered were no longer butchered without thought. Defeated foes were sometimes allowed to live. Violence remained part of who he was—he was still an Orc, still Warsong, still a warrior—but violence without purpose had become abhorrent to him.

CHAPTER 04 ✦ Freedom, and the reckoning it allowed.

When the Rage Went Quiet

When Grom Hellscream finally slew Mannoroth and died freeing the Orcs from the blood curse, Sarthuk felt something he had not experienced since his youth.

Silence.

The rage was still there.

But now it was his.

CHAPTER 05 ✦ Without banners, orders or excuses.

The Long Walk Home

After the Battle of Mount Hyjal, Sarthuk became separated from the surviving Warsong forces.

He did not immediately seek them out.

Instead, he disappeared into the wilderness of Ashenvale and lived as a hermit for many months.

For the first time since Draenor, there were no orders to follow, no banners to march beneath, and no enemies placed before him.

There was only time.

Time to remember.

Time to decide what kind of Orc he intended to become.

CHAPTER 06 ✦ Orgrimmar, Thrall and a purpose worth claiming.

A Different Horde

Eventually, Sarthuk emerged from Ashenvale and travelled south into the Barrens. There he began hearing stories about a new Orcish homeland being built in Durotar.

Orgrimmar.

A city not raised for conquest, but for survival.

A Horde that was trying to become something different from the one Sarthuk had known.

And at its head stood Thrall.

Sarthuk had fought as part of Thrall's Horde in Kalimdor, but he had never truly considered himself Thrall's warrior. His loyalty had always belonged first to Grom, to the Warsong, and to the legacy of the Bloodbane family.

That changed in Orgrimmar.

When Sarthuk finally stood before the young Warchief, he pledged his allegiance not merely to another commander, but to the idea Thrall represented: that the Orcs could acknowledge what they had done without allowing it to define what they would become.

Sarthuk did not seek forgiveness.

Some things could not be forgiven.

He sought purpose.

CHAPTER 07 ✦ Never again would blood decide.

What Bloodbane Means

He had always been Sarthuk Bloodbane, son of a proud Warsong line.

But from that day forward, the name Bloodbane carried new meaning.

To outsiders, it sounded like the name of another brutal Orc warrior.

To Sarthuk, it became a reminder that blood would never rule him again.

He had once been consumed by the curse in Mannoroth's blood.

He had survived it.

He had defied it.

And never again would blood decide what kind of warrior he was.

Sarthuk Bloodbane.

Of the Warsong Clan.

Son of a warrior line.

Veteran of two worlds.

Defender of the Horde.

And when the drums of war sounded again, he answered them as he always had.

Not because he needed to kill.

Because there were things worth fighting for.

For the Horde.
