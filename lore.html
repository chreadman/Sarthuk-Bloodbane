THE LORE

Sarthuk Bloodbane, of the Warsong Clan

AN ORC OF ANOTHER AGE

Sarthuk Bloodbane was forged in the violent years of the old Horde. Born into the traditions of the Warsong Clan, with ties to the harsh Blackrock legacy, he knew an age when an orc's name was earned in battle and kept through blood and iron.

He lived through the shadow of demonic corruption, when the Horde's fury was twisted by forces far greater than any single warrior. The scars of that age never truly faded.

I. The Second War

He fought through the campaigns of the Second War, carrying the fury and discipline of a veteran of the old Horde. He survived what broke lesser warriors, but survival came with its own price.

II. The Third War and Mannoroth

The Third War changed the fate of his people. Mannoroth's fall marked the breaking of the blood curse and a turning point for the orcs. For Sarthuk, freedom from the old chains did not instantly provide a new purpose.

III. The Wanderer

After the demon's death, Sarthuk walked a path of his own. Detached from the noise of glory and command, he wandered, carrying both the victories and the sins of his people with him.

IV. The New Horde

In time, Thrall's Orgrimmar offered something worth returning to: a Horde defined not merely by conquest, but by survival, loyalty and the right to forge its own future. Sarthuk found his place again in its ranks.

V. Defender of Azeroth

Through campaigns and impossible odds, Sarthuk became a frontline warrior, shield-bearer and veteran leader. He was the first through the breach, the last to abandon the line, and never one to mistake responsibility for weakness.

“Blood and thunder. For the Horde.”
